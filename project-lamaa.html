Lamaa — Home Cleaning Company

A company website for a home-cleaning service, with a deep navy palette, an Arabic right-to-left interface and separate pages for services, process and contact.

Role

Design & Development

Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Year

2026

OverView

Lamaa presents a home-cleaning company to its customers: what the company offers, how the service works, reviews, common questions and a way to get in touch.

What I Built

A home page with a hero section, services, a how-we-work process, customer reviews and an FAQ, plus separate services and contact pages with a dropdown menu and a contact form that sends the message through WhatsApp.

The Challenge

I built the site in Arabic with a right-to-left layout and a dark navy palette. The hardest part was making the colors feel natural and keeping the same design across four pages.

What I Learned

I learned to structure a multi-page site, build a dropdown menu and connect a contact form to WhatsApp, and to keep CSS organized as the project grows.
